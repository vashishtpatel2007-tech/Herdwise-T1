How it works

Three things, and none of them are a fence.

  1. STEP 01

    Walk your field

    Hold the phone and walk the edge of your own land. The boundary draws itself. No map reading, no guessing on a satellite photo.

  2. STEP 02

    The collar watches

    It knows the boundary and the highways near it, stored on the device. It keeps working when there is no signal at all.

  3. STEP 03

    It turns her back

    Sound on one ear, then a vibration. She walks away from it — back into your field, before she is anywhere near the road.

What she feels

It only gets louder if she keeps going.

Four levels, and no shock at any of them. A startled animal runs in whatever direction she was already facing — next to a highway, that is the thing you are trying to prevent.

  • 01

    A tone

    She has drifted toward the edge. Most animals turn here.

  • 02

    Faster tone

    She has not turned yet. The sound picks up on one side only.

  • 03

    Tone and vibration

    Under thirty metres from the highway, still closing.

  • 04

    Continuous, both ears

    She is on the road. Now the noise itself may move her — and your phone rings.

On your phone

Move the herd without walking it.

The fence is software, so it can move. Draw tomorrow's grazing on the map and the collars walk them over — no gates, no shouting, no lost afternoon.

  • Working

    Live herd

    Every animal on the satellite map, with how old each position is. Tap one to see her speed, her collar charge, her day's track.

  • Next

    Shift to a new field

    Draw the next paddock, press shift. The collars widen the old boundary and close the new one, and the herd moves itself across.

  • Working

    Grazing hours

    This field, 6am to 6pm, weekdays. Outside those hours the fence stands down — but the highway warnings never do.

  • Working

    Collars off

    Walking them down the lane to another village? Switch the collars off from the app. They keep reporting position; they just stop arguing.

  • Next

    Named fields, on rotation

    Keep several fields and set the week's order. Each morning the right one is live and the rest are resting.

  • Working

    A tag anyone can scan

    Print a tag for her ear. Whoever finds her — any phone, no app, no account — gets a button that calls you.

What it does not do.

Every one of these is a decision, not a missing feature.

  • —It never shocks her. Sound and vibration only, at every level.
  • —It stays quiet along a roadside. An animal grazing parallel to a highway is not warned, however close. Warn her there and she learns the collar means nothing.
  • —It says nothing when it cannot see the sky. A poor GPS fix raises a collar fault, never a false alarm about your animal.
  • —It never shows an old position as if it were now. Every location on the screen carries how old it is.
